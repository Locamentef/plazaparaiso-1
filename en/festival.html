What is Plaza Paraíso, the Torremolinos festival

One summer. A historic venue. Four ways to enjoy it. This is Plaza Paraíso.

How to get there
The meeting point of summer

The Plaza de Toros
is transformed

This summer 2026, the historic Plaza de Toros de Torremolinos becomes the great meeting point of culture. For more than 50 days, its ring fills with concerts, food and leisure in a unique open-air setting, a stone's throw from the beach.

Plaza Paraíso was born for locals from Torremolinos and Málaga, and for anyone spending the summer on the Costa del Sol looking for something different. An open, welcoming, summery date to live the best evenings of the season.

How to get there

From Málaga centre to the plaza

The Plaza de Toros is a stone's throw from Málaga city. Here are the simplest ways to get there in the evening and back home in the small hours.

There
Málaga centre → Plaza de Toros
Leave ~17:45 · arrive around 18:30

Cercanías C-1 train

Most comfortable
  • ~22 min
  • ~€2
  • Every ~20 min

Departs from Málaga Centro-Alameda. Get off at Torremolinos station; from there, it's 1.5 km to the plaza (~18 min on foot or a short taxi ride). Tickets from the machines or the Renfe app.

Bus M-110

  • ~35-45 min
  • ~€1.55

Direct from Muelle Heredia / Alameda Principal to Torremolinos. Stops in the town centre; get off at the stop closest to the plaza and walk from there.

Taxi · Cabify · Uber

  • ~15-20 min
  • Taxi €20-28

Door to door from anywhere in the centre. Cabify/Uber, similar price or slightly lower, book from the app.

Back
Plaza de Toros → Málaga centre
Departure around 01:00

Bus M-110

Only public option
  • ~40-50 min
  • ~€1.55

On Saturdays it runs until 03:00, so there's a service at 1:00. Closest stop in central Torremolinos (Av. Palma de Mallorca), ~10 min on foot. Exact times at ctmam.es or the CTMAM app.

Taxi

Recommended
  • €25-35

Pick-up point: Calle Los Nidos / Av. del Real, opposite the plaza, or the nearest roundabout with space to stop.

Cercanías C-1 train

Not available

The last train from Torremolinos to Málaga leaves around 23:30. By 1:00 there's no more service.

Times and prices are indicative for a summer Saturday in 2026. Always check the day's schedule on Renfe Cercanías and ctmam.es before you head out.
